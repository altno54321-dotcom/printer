How to print double-sided

  1. 1
    Print Part A: Download Part A and print it using your normal single-sided printer settings.
  2. 2
    Preserve Stack Order: Keep the printed sheets in their exact output stack order. Do not shuffle or reorder the pages.
  3. 3
    Flip & Reinsert: Flip and reinsert the printed stack into your paper tray according to your printer's specific paper-feed mechanism.
  4. 4
    Print Part B: Download Part B and print it onto the reverse sides of the reinserted sheets.
  5. 5
    Verify Finished Document: Inspect your final document to ensure pages are correctly aligned and sequential from front to back.
Important Printer Variation Notice:

Every printer feed mechanism functions differently (front vs. top loading, face-up vs. face-down output trays). We strongly recommend running a small 4-page test document first to identify your printer's exact paper flip direction before printing long projects.
